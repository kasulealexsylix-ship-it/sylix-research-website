Frequently asked questions

What you may want to know before starting

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. The initial discussion helps us understand your project, identify the most suitable support and prepare a quotation before you commit.

How is the project price determined?

The quotation depends on the scope, urgency, level of study, available materials, complexity of the analysis and the support required. Prices are not displayed because projects differ significantly.

Can you support clients outside Uganda?

Yes. Support can be provided online through email, WhatsApp and virtual meetings to clients in East Africa and internationally.

Will my documents remain confidential?

Yes. Client documents and project information are handled privately and used only for the agreed work.

Do you make revisions?

Agreed revisions are handled within the defined project scope. A major change in topic, methodology or deliverables may require a revised quotation.

How do I send my documents?

Submit the request form first, then send your documents through WhatsApp or email. You will receive confirmation after the files are reviewed.
